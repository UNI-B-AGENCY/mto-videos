About Nsom

Nsom Emmanuel Afumbom

Founder & Digital Business Consultant

Nsom Emmanuel Afumbom is a software entrepreneur, digital strategist, business consultant, and technology leader with experience building digital products, advising businesses, and helping organisations use technology to solve practical problems.

As the Founder and Director of Uni B Agency, Emmanuel works at the intersection of technology, digital marketing, business strategy, and entrepreneurship. He advises businesses and founders on areas including digital transformation, product and business idea validation, market opportunities, audience development, digital marketing, online visibility, and scaling.

His experience is not built solely from theory. Over the years, he has founded and worked on multiple technology and business ventures, some of which succeeded and others which did not. These experiences have given him a practical understanding of what happens after an idea leaves the notebook and enters the real market.

He has also built and led initiatives across technology, youth development, healthcare, and entrepreneurship. He has also organised and led international programmes, working with people and organisations across different countries and backgrounds.

Emmanuel holds multiple academic qualifications, including a Master's degree in Computer Software Engineering, and combines his technical background with practical business experience.

His approach to consulting is simple: understand the problem, understand the market, understand the people, and then build what makes sense.

Through Uni B Agency, he continues to help businesses and organisations turn ideas into practical digital solutions, build stronger brands, reach the right audiences, and create sustainable growth.
